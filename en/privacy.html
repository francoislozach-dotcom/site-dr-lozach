Browsing and hosting

The host receives technical connection information including IP address, date and requested resources to operate and secure the site, based on the legitimate interest in providing a reliable service. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts; this connection sends your IP address to Google.

Optional statistics

Google Analytics loads only if you accept statistics. It uses cookies to measure page visits and browser and device characteristics. Processing is based on consent; refusing does not restrict access. Advertising personalisation and Google Signals are disabled.

Your choice is stored in your browser for 180 days. Analytics cookies are configured to expire within 180 days without automatic renewal on each visit. You can withdraw consent at any time using “Cookie settings”: measurement is disabled in this browser and accessible Analytics cookies are deleted. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Google receives measurement information and may process it outside the European Union under its terms. See Google’s privacy policy for recipients, transfers and safeguards.

Appointments and correspondence

Appointment links open Doctolib, a separate service with its own privacy policy. Telephone and email links open your usual applications. Please ask the practice for an appropriate channel before sending medical records.

Your rights
