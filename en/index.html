◍ Approach

The right move,
not one move too many.

Every joint has its own story. My role is to understand yours — your lifestyle, your pain, your expectations — before proposing a solution.

The best surgery is the one you can avoid. When surgery becomes the best option, I use the least invasive techniques: mini-invasive approaches, arthroscopy, enhanced recovery protocols. The goal is not simply to operate — it is to get you back on your feet, quickly and effectively.
