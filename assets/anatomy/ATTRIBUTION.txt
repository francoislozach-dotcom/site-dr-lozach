Adaptation for drlozach.fr: selection of 40 skeletal structures, 35 peripheral
nerve structures and 58 muscles of the right lower limb; original mesh geometry
and coordinates retained. Rendering changes: yellow nerves, blue-grey bones,
transparent muscles, regional camera crops, automatic rotation.
The derived anatomical model files and rendered anatomical views are provided
under CC BY-SA 4.0. This exception is separate from the site's own text and other
assets. No book illustration or operative photograph is copied into these models.
An educational atlas is not a patient-specific surgical navigation model.
